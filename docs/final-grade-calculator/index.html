How to use it

  1. Current grade: your grade in the class right now, before the final. Most online gradebooks show this.
  2. Final exam weight: how much the final counts toward your course grade. Look for it in the syllabus.
  3. Grade you want: the overall course percentage you're aiming for.

The formula

Your course grade after the final is a weighted mix of your current grade and your final exam score:

Course grade = current × (1 − w) + final × w, where w is the final's weight as a decimal.

Solving for the final score gives:

Final needed = (target − current × (1 − w)) ÷ w

Example: with an 84% now, a final worth 25% and a target of 90%, you need (90 − 84 × 0.75) ÷ 0.25 = 108% — out of reach without extra credit, but an 80% target only needs 68%.

FAQ

What if the result is over 100%?
That target can't be reached with the final alone. Ask whether there's extra credit, or aim for the next grade down — the table shows every letter grade at once.
What if it's below 0%?
You'll reach that grade even with a zero on the final. (Still take it — many classes require it to pass.)
My class uses points, not percentages.
Divide the points you have by the points possible so far to get your current percentage, and use the final's share of total points as its weight.
