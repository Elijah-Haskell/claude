How to use the GPA calculator

  1. Enter each course's credit hours and the letter grade you earned (or expect).
  2. Your semester GPA updates as you type.
  3. To see your cumulative GPA, add your current GPA and how many credits you've already completed.

Your courses are saved in this browser only, so they'll still be here if you come back.

How GPA is calculated

Each letter grade is worth a number of grade points. Multiply each course's grade points by its credits, add those up, then divide by the total credits:

GPA = Σ (grade points × credits) ÷ Σ credits

Cumulative GPA works the same way, combining the grade points from your past credits (current GPA × credits completed) with this semester's.

FAQ

Do pass/fail courses count?
Usually not. Pass/fail (or credit/no-credit) courses earn credits but no grade points at most schools, so leave them out.
What about retaken courses?
Policies differ. Many schools replace the old grade with the new one; others average both. Enter only the grades your school counts.
Is a weighted (5.0) high-school GPA supported?
Not yet — this calculator uses the unweighted 4.0 scale.
