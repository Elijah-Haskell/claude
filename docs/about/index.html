About

Semester Toolkit is a collection of free, simple tools for students — grade calculators, citation help, a word counter, a study timer and more.

Every tool:

  • Runs in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
  • Is free and needs no account. The site is supported by advertising.
  • Shows its working. Each page explains the formula or rules it uses, so you can check the result yourself.

New tools are added regularly. If a result looks wrong or there's a tool you'd like, please get in touch.
