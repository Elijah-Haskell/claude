What this word counter measures

  • Words: anything separated by spaces that contains a letter or number, so stray dashes and symbols aren't counted.
  • Characters: every character including spaces, and a second count without spaces — useful for applications with character limits.
  • Sentences and paragraphs: sentences end in . ! or ?; paragraphs are separated by a blank line.
  • Most repeated words: words of three or more letters you've used more than once (common words like “the” are skipped) — handy for spotting repetition in an essay.

How reading and speaking time are estimated

Reading time uses 238 words per minute by default. That's the average silent reading rate for English non-fiction found in a review of 190 studies (Brysbaert, 2019, Journal of Memory and Language). Speaking time defaults to 140 words per minute, a steady presentation pace. Change either number to match how fast you read or talk.

FAQ

Does my text get uploaded?
No. Counting happens entirely in your browser, and your text isn't saved.
Why is my count different from Word or Google Docs?
Tools treat hyphenated words, numbers and symbols slightly differently. The difference is usually a handful of words.
