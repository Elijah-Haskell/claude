How to use it

Add one row per category from your syllabus — for example Homework 20%, Quizzes 15%, Midterm 25%, Final 40% — and type your average score in each. Leave the score blank for categories you haven't been graded on yet.

How a weighted grade is calculated

Multiply each category's score by its weight, add them up, and divide by the total weight of the categories you've been graded in:

Weighted grade = Σ (score × weight) ÷ Σ weight

Dividing by the weight you've actually completed means the result is your grade so far. Once every category has a score and the weights total 100%, it's your final course grade.

Letter grades

The letter uses a common scale: A 93+, A− 90, B+ 87, B 83, B− 80, C+ 77, C 73, C− 70, D+ 67, D 63, D− 60, F below 60. Your instructor's cutoffs may differ.

FAQ

My weights add up to more than 100%.
Double-check the syllabus — some classes list bonus categories separately. The calculator still divides by the total weight, so the result stays on a 0–100 scale.
My class uses points instead of weights.
Then every point counts equally: divide total points earned by total points possible. You don't need weights at all.
