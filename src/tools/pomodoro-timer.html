What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management method created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. You work in short, focused blocks — traditionally 25 minutes — with a short break after each one and a longer break after every four. Knowing a break is coming makes it easier to start hard work and to ignore distractions until then.

How to study with this timer

  1. Pick one task — a problem set, a chapter, a draft section.
  2. Press Start and work only on that task until the alarm.
  3. Take the break. Stand up, get water, look away from the screen.
  4. After four focus sessions, take the long break.

FAQ

Can I change the lengths?
Yes. Many students prefer 50-minute focus blocks with 10-minute breaks for deep work. Your settings are remembered in this browser.
Does the timer keep running in a background tab?
Yes. It tracks the actual end time, so it stays accurate even if your browser slows down background tabs.
I don't hear the alarm.
Browsers only allow sound after you've clicked on the page, and your device must not be muted.
