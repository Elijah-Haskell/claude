How to use the study planner

  1. Set the exam date. The start date defaults to today.
  2. Estimate the total hours you need. A rough guide: add up the chapters or topics and give each a realistic amount of time.
  3. Tick any days you won't study. The hours are spread evenly over the days that are left.

The exam day itself isn't counted as a study day.

Tips for spacing your studying

  • Start early and spread it out. Several shorter sessions on different days usually beat one long cram session for remembering material.
  • Test yourself. Practice questions and flashcards work better than re-reading notes.
  • Leave a buffer. Plan to finish a day or two before the exam so a busy day doesn't sink the plan.

FAQ

Can I plan several exams at once?
Not yet — plan each one separately and add the daily hours together.
Is my plan saved?
Your exam date, hours and days off are saved in this browser only.
